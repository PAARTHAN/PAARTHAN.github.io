About me

I'm Paarthan. I like building the tool instead of waiting for someone else to, and then getting people to actually use it.

During my Computer Science degree at R.M.K. Engineering College in Chennai, I founded Code on JVM in October 2024. It grew to more than 6,000 Java and Kotlin developers, and I directed over 25 meetups, hackathons and workshops, managing speakers, sponsors, venues and a volunteer team. Alongside that I interned as an Associate Product Manager at TIXON and as an Engineering Intern at Excelencia Consulting, where I built Galaxio.

Now in Dublin, my cohort of 120+ elected me Class Ambassador, and I built them Smurfit Class Hub so nobody misses a deadline again. Why F1? I spend race weekends analysing strategy and telemetry, and I build software the same way: test on Friday, tune on Saturday, deliver on Sunday.
